# Legacy configurations

These files preserve historical experiments, fixed-power paper checks, and
the former split geometry/power inputs. They are not active canonical cases.

Active research entry points are documented in `configs/README.md`.
Literature fixed-power inputs retained here remain reference/history data
rather than analytical memory-power sources.

`unvalidated/` contains structurally loadable research branches that are not
members of the active comparison. In particular, `orthogonal_m3d_si.yaml`
has no validated memory-power primitive and must not be presented as the
Orthogonal M3D-IGZO proposed design or as a fourth canonical case.
